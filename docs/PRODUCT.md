# Product context

Rivertide helps seasonal small-business owners understand future cash and test a purchase before committing. Initial audiences: restaurants, landscaping crews, retail shops and charters. Assume little financial training; explain outcomes in plain language beside the relevant numbers.

## Core journey
See a working sample → change purchase amount/timing → understand lowest projected cash and cushion → optionally enter personal estimates → leave feedback. Keep sample data clearly labeled. Plans currently save in the browser; do not imply account sync or bank connectivity.

## Launch direction
Open self-service demo, collect unfiltered owner feedback, iterate, then consider paid beta. No closed-pilot prerequisite or fixed launch date. TikTok demonstrations and targeted outreach are proposed acquisition channels, not authorization to send messages or spend money.

## Boundaries
Do not add integrations, AI spreadsheet interpretation, browser extensions, business-sale preparation, billing or testimonials without scoped approval. Do not invent founder details, customers or guarantees. Keep mission concise; do not reintroduce founder portrait/student biography as marketing proof without direction.

App repo owns this product contract. Marketing mirrors it; update both deliberately when product decisions change.
