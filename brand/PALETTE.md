# Rivertide color standard

Owner approved monochrome across website and app on November 19, 2026 after reviewing interactive black-and-white previews.

Use black/charcoal surfaces, white text, gray secondary text and light-gray selected controls. Shared design CSS must remain byte-identical across both repositories. The document grayscale rule also neutralizes legacy hard-coded chart colors and imagery, matching the approved preview.

Preserve Public Sans, approved Signature R artwork, transparent logo backgrounds, geometry and forecast calculations. Preserve solid/dashed chart lines, outlined/filled markers and explicit warning copy so meaning never depends on color. Do not restore blue/ice accents or red warnings without owner direction.
